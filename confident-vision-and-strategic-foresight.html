Confident Vision and Strategic Foresight

The extraordinary ability of Julio Herrera Velutini to anticipate changes in the market and place his businesses to profit from those shifts is one of his distinguishing features. He was successful in his mission to transform Britannia Financial Group and Bancredito International Bank & Trust Corporation into globally competitive institutions.

Since Velutini thinks financial services should be creative and personalized for each client, he often reinterprets industry standards instead of blindly following them. His system combines time-tested ideas with state-of-the-art tools, which is why his customers have faith in his items. His competitors keep a careful eye on him.
